How the difficulty scales

  • More circles — starting at 3, one more is added after every cleared round.
  • Smaller targets — circle radius shrinks with each level, forcing precise aim.
  • Less memorizing time — the numbers stay visible for fewer milliseconds every round.

Scoring

Every correctly-typed circle is worth 100 points. Deductions: 30 per mistake, plus a small speed penalty when a level takes longer than the target pace. Finish fast and clean to climb.

Your best score is saved locally in your browser — no account, no leaderboard, no tracking.

Works with mouse, trackpad or touch. The mechanic combines osu!-style circular aiming with a sequence-memory task: big aiming win, bigger memory win.

Frequently asked questions

Is this training actually useful?

It exercises two skills gamers rely on: precise cursor placement under time pressure, and short-term spatial memory. It won’t replace real aim trainers, but it’s an engaging benchmark for both.

Why do the circles get smaller?

Reduced target size is the standard difficulty lever for aim precision. As radius shrinks, the same distance error becomes a miss, so you must place each click more accurately.

How do I get a good score?

Memorize the order in the first pass (scan left-to-right by number), then click decisively. Fewer mistakes matter more than raw speed — each error costs 30 points.

Does my result get uploaded?

No. Everything runs and stays in your browser. Best scores are stored only on your own device.
