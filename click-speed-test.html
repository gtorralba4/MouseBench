What is CPS?

CPS (clicks per second) counts mouse clicks delivered in one second. CPM is the same number over a minute. Scores above 7–8 CPS are fast for ordinary clicking; 10+ usually means jitter-clicking or butterfly-clicking technique.

How to get the most accurate result

  • Use a physical mouse rather than a trackpad.
  • Go as hard as you can for 5 seconds first; long durations test endurance more than peak speed.
  • Results are measured from the browser events, identical to how games register clicks.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CPS score?

4–6 CPS is average, 7–9 is above average, and beyond 10 usually requires a specific clicking technique (jitter or butterfly). Any result is learnable — technique trumps hardware at these speeds.

Does the click speed test use the server?

No. All timing and counting happens locally with the browser timer, which is why results appear instantly.

Which mouse button should I use?

Use the left button for a consistent comparison. High-end mice with lighter switches and faster polling (500–1000 Hz) tend to register rapid clicks more reliably.
