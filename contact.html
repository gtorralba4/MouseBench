Contact

Something unclear, a test misbehaving, or a guide you wish existed? Write to us. We answer every serious message.

Email us

The fastest way to reach the team is email. We keep replies short and usually answer within a few days.

Write to

hello@mousebench.fyi

What to include

The more context, the faster we can help. For bug reports please include your mouse model, computer operating system, browser name and version, and one or two sentences about what you expected versus what happened.

  • Mouse model and connection type (wired / dongle / Bluetooth).
  • Operating system and browser version.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if it is a bug.

What happens next

We read every message and answer personally. If you report an issue with a tool, we test it, fix it if we can reproduce it, and let you know when the update is live.

Frequently asked questions

Do you reply to all messages?

Yes. Personalised replies go out for every serious message, usually within two or three days.

Can I request a new tool?

Absolutely. Tell us what you would like to test, and we will build it if it is technically possible in a browser.

Is support free?

Yes. Asking questions, reporting bugs and requesting features costs nothing.
