How to Fix a Mouse That Double Clicks

A mouse that registers two clicks when you press once is one of the most common mouse faults. Before you buy a new one, try these fixes that solve the problem in most cases.

Why does a mouse start double-clicking?

The main button of a mouse contains a small component called a microswitch. Every time you press it, the internal metal contact bends, and after thousands of clicks the contact material wears down. When it loses its tension, the switch produces a bounce that registers as two clicks. Dirt, humidity and age make it worse.

Rule out software problems first

Before touching the hardware, rule out software causes. Disconnect the mouse and plug it into another USB port. If the fault only appears in some programs, the problem is in the software, not the mouse.

  • Check the Windows double-click speed and click lock under Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse.
  • Update the mouse driver in Device Manager, then install the manufacturer software if available (Logitech G HUB, Razer Synapse, etc.).
  • Compare with a second mouse in the same program to confirm the hardware is the problem.

Adjust the double-click speed or debounce time

  1. Reset the Windows double-click speed to the middle. A value set too low makes every click count as a double-click.
  2. In gaming mice, increase the debounce time in the mouse software. A debounce of 8-10 ms removes most false bounces.
  3. Re-test with the free CPS test to see if the phantom second click is gone.

Clean the switch without opening the mouse

Many double-click faults are caused by a dirty or lightly worn contact. Blow compressed air around the button gap, then press the button rapidly about 30 to 50 times. This simple mechanical cleaning fixes temporary faults in about half of all cases.

Replace the microswitch, the durable fix

If cleaning does not help, the switch metals are worn out. If the mouse is under warranty, claim it first. Otherwise open the mouse, desolder the old switch and solder in a new one. This is a repair for people with soldering experience; everyone else should weigh the cost of a service against a new mouse.

  • Common switches: Omron D2FC-F-7N, Kailh GM8.0, Huano Blue Shell.
  • Heat the solder pads, remove the switch, place the new one, then verify with the Mouse Button Test.
  • If you are not confident soldering, a service center or a new mouse is the safer choice.

When to buy a new mouse

If the switch fails again a few months after repair, the whole switch group is usually worn. Budget mice are often cheaper to replace than to repair. When buying, look for switches rated for 50 million clicks or more, and check the warranty terms before opening anything.

Frequently asked questions

Can a double-click be fixed with software?

Often yes. Increasing the debounce time in gaming mouse software, or lowering the Windows double-click speed, removes many phantom second clicks on worn switches.

How long does a mouse switch last?

Quality switches are rated for 20 to 100 million clicks. Under heavy gaming the left button switch is usually the first to fail.

Is it safe to open my mouse to clean it?

For most wired and wireless mice, yes, but opening it usually voids the warranty. Clean it only when it is out of warranty or you are sure about what you are doing.
