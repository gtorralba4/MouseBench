Privacy Policy

Short version: no downloads, no account. The tests run entirely in your browser; advertising may use cookies only with your consent.

No downloads

Every tool on MouseBench runs as a normal web page. There is nothing to install, no plug-in, and no software to download — you just open a page and start testing.

No account

You don’t need to register, sign up, or log in to use any of the tests. There are no accounts, no profiles, and no saved scores on our side.

No data gathering

MouseBench does not track you, does not collect your results, and does not transmit anything. The tests process your mouse events locally with standard browser APIs, and the measurement starts and ends inside your current tab. You can confirm this yourself: open your browser’s developer tools, switch to the network panel, and run any test — you will see that no network requests are made.

What’s stored on your device

The only thing MouseBench saves is a small preference on your own device: which theme (light or dark) you picked. That value is kept in your browser’s local storage, used only to show the page the way you like it, and is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

MouseBench uses Google AdSense to display advertising. AdSense is a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited if you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland) that may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads that are more relevant to you. When advertising cookies are active, Google may use your prior visits to MouseBench and to other websites to serve personalised ads. If you don’t want personalised advertising, Google shows you non-personalised ads instead, based on the choice you make on our consent banner; you can also disable personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings.

Advertising cookies are used to measure the effectiveness of ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and record the pages you visit so you don’t see the same ad everywhere. To do this, Google may process data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you open, and advertising event data (for example, whether you clicked an ad). Google’s use of advertising cookies is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and its advertising technologies page.

Your consent (GDPR / UK)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising cookies are only placed after you give your consent. When you visit the site, a consent banner from Google’s consent management platform lets you choose Consent (all cookies), Don’t consent (no advertising cookies), or Manage options to decide in detail. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the “Manage options” (privacy) control on the site. If you choose not to consent, you’ll still see advertising, but it will be non-personalised (for example, not based on your previous browsing).

You can also opt out of personalised advertising by third-party vendors at aboutads.info and via YourOnlineChoices.
