Privacy Policy

Short version: no downloads, no account, no data gathering. Every test runs entirely in your browser and nothing you do here leaves your device.

Ad slot · Leaderboard (728×90)

No downloads

Every tool on MouseBench runs as a normal web page. There is nothing to install, no plug-in, and no software to download — you just open a page and start testing.

No account

You don’t need to register, sign up, or log in to use any of the tests. There are no accounts, no profiles, and no saved scores on our side.

No data gathering

MouseBench does not track you, does not collect your results, and does not transmit anything. The tests process your mouse events locally with standard browser APIs, and the measurement starts and ends inside your current tab. You can confirm this yourself: open your browser’s developer tools, switch to the network panel, and run any test — you will see that no network requests are made.

What’s stored on your device

The only thing MouseBench saves is a small preference on your own device: which theme (light or dark) you picked. That value is kept in your browser’s local storage, used only to show the page the way you like it, and is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

Reserved spaces on this site may carry advertising in the future. If an advertising network is enabled, it processes its own data under its own privacy policy, and this page will be updated before that happens.

Changes to this policy

If anything in this policy changes, we’ll update it here and refresh the date below. Current version: September 2026.
