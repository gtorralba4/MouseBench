What Is a Good CPS?

A good CPS depends on your age, what you are doing, and how much you train. Here are real benchmarks so you can see where your score fits.

Average click speed for most people

A typical person clicks between 5 and 7 clicks per second on a short test. Casual users settle around 4 to 5 CPS, while players who use mice daily reach 7 to 9 CPS without special training. These are normal ranges, not extremes.

  • 4-5 CPS: typical for everyday and office use.
  • 6-7 CPS: common among students and casual gamers.
  • 8-10 CPS: reached by regular gamers and trained clickers.

Does age matter for clicking speed?

Age has a smaller effect on raw click speed than on reaction time. Reflexes peak in the late teens to early twenties, but clicking skill is mostly muscle memory, so a trained adult often clicks faster than an untrained teenager.

  • Children (8-12): often 4-6 CPS, quickly trainable.
  • Teens (13-19): the fastest group, 6-9 CPS on average.
  • Adults (20-40): 5-7 CPS with training maintaining high scores.
  • Older adults (40+): 4-6 CPS, still improving with practice.

What competitive clickers reach

Dedicated CPS testers and content creators reach 10 to 12 CPS with the index finger, and some push toward 14 to 15 CPS by alternating fingers or using a light jitter technique. Anything above 12 CPS within a single finger is exceptional.

How to measure your own CPS

Use a reliable CPS test that counts real clicks over a set time. Set the timer to 5 seconds for your max speed and to 60 seconds for your sustained average. Take three runs and keep the best score as your real result.

  1. Open the click speed test and set it to 5 seconds.
  2. Click as fast as you can while keeping the button fully pressed each time.
  3. Repeat twice more and keep the highest CPS value.

How to improve your CPS score

Clicking speed responds to practice. Keep your finger close to the button, use the wrist for short, quick presses, and train in short bursts of 30 to 60 seconds a day. Sustain training matters more than long sessions.

  • Keep your hand relaxed and the finger near the button.
  • Shake your wrist slightly instead of moving the whole arm.
  • Practice 5-second bursts, then increase to 10 seconds.
  • Try your index and middle finger to find your fastest.

Frequently asked questions

Is 7 CPS good?

Yes. Seven clicks per second is above average and is a solid score for any purpose, including most click games.

What is the maximum possible CPS?

With a single finger, 15 CPS is about the practical limit for brief bursts. Sustained rates are lower; the world-class records sit around 15 CPS average over 5 to 30 seconds.

Do gaming mice make you click faster?

Not magically. Lightweight buttons and low debounce let trained fingers go slightly faster, but your skill determines most of the score.
